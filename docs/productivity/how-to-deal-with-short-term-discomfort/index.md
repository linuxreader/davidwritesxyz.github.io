# How to Deal With Short Term Discomfort

You will never outpace your vision for the future. Create things mentally before you create them physically.  
  
Engage your thinking about what is possible for you. This makes your vision for the future compelling enough to act on.  
  
Vision creates the emotional connection and passion needed to act on your goals.  
  
Short term discomfort then just becomes the reps needed to establish your vision.  
  
Just like lifting weights at the gym.
