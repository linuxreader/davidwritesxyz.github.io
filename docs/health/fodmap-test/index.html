Based on all the foods across your logs, here’s a tier ranking. Portion matters a lot for some of these (noted where relevant) — Monash University’s testing is the standard reference for FODMAP levels.

Very high FODMAP

  • Dried mango, raisins (concentrated fructose + sorbitol)
  • Watermelon (large portions, 300g+)
  • Garlic (garlic bread, sauces)
  • Cooked onion (more than a couple tablespoons)
  • Red lentil pasta / lentils
  • Chickpeas (canned helps, but still moderate-high in normal servings)
  • Wheat bread, bagels, tortillas, biscuits (fructans)
  • Erythritol (protein powder) — its own polyol category
  • Brussels sprouts

High FODMAP

  • Sweet potato (large portions, 250g+)
  • Grapes (large portions, 400g+)
  • Very ripe bananas
  • Apples with skin
  • Pickled onion
  • Edamame (larger portions)
  • Refried beans

Moderate / portion-dependent

  • Almonds, pistachios, cashews (small handful = fine, larger amounts climb)
  • Quinoa (typically fine in normal servings)
  • Broccoli, raw (moderate; cooked is gentler)
  • Sweet potato (small portions, under ~100g)
  • Tempeh (moderate; fermentation helps some)
  • Cauliflower/crucifer-adjacent sauces or blends
  • Green beans (moderate)
  • Tomato (larger amounts)

Low FODMAP

  • Rolled oats (standard serving)
  • Rice, sushi rice, quinoa
  • Firm tofu
  • Blueberries, strawberries, pineapple, oranges, unripe/green banana
  • Carrots, cucumber, bell peppers, celery, kale, watermelon radish
  • Potato (white, red, plain)
  • Hemp hearts, brazil nuts (in your typical small amounts)
  • Peanut butter powder (PBfit)
  • Nutritional yeast
  • Olive oil
  • Chicken breast, plant-based meat alternatives (generally low, check individual ingredient lists for onion/garlic powder additives)
  • Popcorn (plain, moderate portion)

Very low / essentially FODMAP-free

  • Soy milk made from soy protein/isolate (not whole soybean) — the kind you’re using (Silk unsweetened) is low FODMAP
  • Ketchup, mustard, most vinegar-based condiments in small amounts
  • White rice noodles/pasta (in contrast to wheat pasta)

A few specific callouts from your log:

  • Your most frequent very-high-FODMAP items by far are the daily erythritol protein powder and the very common dried mango — these show up more often than anything else on this list
  • Wheat pasta (macaroni, spaghetti) appears often and is a meaningful fructan source you might not think of as “high FODMAP” the way onion or garlic feels obvious
  • Garlic bread (9-2) is one of the single most concentrated FODMAP items you’ve logged, even in a small serving
