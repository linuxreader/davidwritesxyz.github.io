History of my caffeine intake

I have struggled to get off caffeine for years. My caffeine intake is a number I have been tracking in Obsidian for two years:

I have actually been serial tapering since 2012. But have not kept a long term record of the numbers. If I had to guess though, the numbers would look something like this:

2012: 1,500mg 2013: 800mg 2014: 400mg 2015: 200mg 2016: 0-200mg 2017: 200mg 2018: 200mg 2019: 200mg 2020: 250mg 2021: 300mg 2022: 350mg 2023: 400mg 2024: 200-400mg 2025: 150-450mg 2026: 250-450mg

That’s just the time that I’ve been “trying to quit”. I’ve actually been a lifelong consumer of caffeine.

What I want to accomplish by quitting caffeine

In order to finally quit, this time has to be different. I’ll note here the things I’ve tried, and why I think I have failed in the past. Then I’ll need to run experiements.

What do I want to acheive by quitting caffeine?

  1. I want better sleep.
  2. I want to be less stressed.
  3. I want better ess muscle tension.
  4. I want better digestion.
  5. I want more stable energy levels.
  6. I want better creativity.
  7. I don’t want to be dependent on any substances.
  8. I want to see if the health gains from quitting caffeine raises my testosterone levels.

What usually leads me to relapse?

Poor sleep > Using caffeine to get through the day. Using caffeine to drive when I am tired.
