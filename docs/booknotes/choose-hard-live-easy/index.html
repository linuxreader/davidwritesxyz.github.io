My notes

The more comfortable you get with temporary discomfort, the more comfortable life becomes.

Choose hard and live an easy life. Or choose easy and live a hard life.

The easy/hard paradox Thinking that choosing easy will give you and easy life.

The 4 rules of engagement

1. Polarity What you repeatedly choose short term will get you the opposite long term.

2. Transience Immediate pain or pleasure is often short-lived.

3. Persistence What ever you repeatedly choose short term, you will eventually live it’s polarity long term.

4. Imminence Hard is inevitable, You can choose it now on your terms, or it will choose you later on it’s terms.

We all must suffer from one of two pains, the pain of discipline or the pain of regret.

Alwayes choose the option that has more short-term pain.

Asymmetry

Choosing hard let’s you weild asymmetry.

Choose easy and you are betting dollars to win dimes. Choose hard and you are betting dimes to win dollars.

5 Decision Powers

Carpe Momentum: Seize the moment.

Focus on winning each moment. Not the day.

1. Momentality

The moment that decisions are made. Your entire life is decided in one moment at a time. Where all decisions are made or not made.

Over an 80 year life you get 252,000,000 ten second moments.

10 seconds is all it takes to change your life or send it to a ditch.

The moment is life’s most precious and parishable currency.

8,600 moments each day.

The 5 choices you can make each moment

1. Neutral flow Decisions that rely on routine or habits the keep the status quo. Sleeping, taking out the trash, etc.

2. Power Hard choices. Uncomfortable investments in your future self. The seed of winning habits and realized dreams.

3.Power Flow Hard choice that becomes a good habit. Choosing hard that has become so routine that it is no longer hard.

4. Treason Feel-good pay later. Easy choices that make your future self bear the consequences.

Subclass: Nuclear Treason Betrayals so big they can ruin your life in a single catastrophic decision.

A one-way ticket to ruin.

5. Treason Flow Where dream suffocate and die. Where your health is slowly poisoned. Where your relationships slowly wither from neglect. Easy choices that are hardened into bad habits. Daily, automated treason against the life you could be living.

Command distribution.

Conscript 10% power/power flow - Minimal growth or investment. 60% Treason – Constant indulgence 30% Neutral - Just existing Result: A life of debt, disease, and regret.

Commander 60% Power/Power Flow - Dominant investment in the future. 30% Neutral - Maintenance of the machine. 10% Treason - Calculated, contained vices that do not threaten the stronghold. Result: Life of wealth, vitality, and freedom. Exponential growth.

Everything significant starts insignificantly. Big things are built with small actions.

2. Eventuality

Where you suffer or thrive from decisions. You live in the skin you sew. Where all of the easy decisions force you to live a hard life. Or where hard decisions let you live an easy life.

3. Pastuality

The past moments that influence your current behavior. How we interpret the past can serve or sabotage us.

The sands of the past lie dormant until you revive them to either server or sabotage you.

Rear view mirrors are for glances, not gazes.

If you want to control your future, look ahead, not behind. Start owning the past. Or let it own you.

Sabotage pastuality

We narrate the past to justify easy choices and to dismiss hard choices.

Service pastuality

The practice of reframing memories to strengthen you in the moment to encourage a better future. Rather then letting memories become an unexamined liability.

R.A.D.A.R

The antidote to Sabotage pastuality.

Reveal > Audit > Disprove > Adjust > Repeat

R. Reveal the big ugly You can’t rewrite a story you’ve buried. Dig up the big ugly memory that has you in paper handcuffs.

A. Audit the story Crack open the decision corpse and expose the disease of easy.

What emotions stomped on logic? Did you buy the hype? Where were the blind spots? Did you skip your due diligence?

Examine overlooked assumptions that led to the ambush. By the end of this audit, you should clearly see which impulses and blind spots led to your downfall.

D. Disprove Reveal yourself as a liar. Find proof that your self-criticism is just an emotional opinion. Find stories that challenge your rigid beliefs. Find case studies who prove that mistakes aren’t life sentences. Every “lessons learned” chips away at the myth you’ve cursed yourself with.

A. Adjust with “next time” or “new meaning”. Transform sabotage into service. What did you learn and what will your change?

Reclaim your power by choosing a new meaning. Accept the truth, then find the higher purpose buried in the wreckage.

Acknowledge the loss. Declare the account closed, then burn it.

R. Repeat Run RADAR indefinitely.

4. Asymmetry

The many future decisions where the transformative power of your decisions hides in eventuality.

Your choices don’t define your life directly, but their outsizes effects do. Either through sudden powerful impacts or gradual wear.

Defining moments of Nuclear treason trigger an immediate, explosive chain reaction that ripple through eventuality: Nuclear Asymmetry.

This explosive out come is a Downside Asymmetric Risk Event (DARE) event.

A single choice can not only destroy your future, but it can also undo every decision that you’ve made in your past.

Easy is how you pull the pin on a DARE.

Flaw of Asymmetry Decision-making is unfairly rigged and favors the downside

Positive compound asymmetry Health elixir

  • Tumeric
  • Hemp seeds
  • Chlorella
  • Spirulina
  • Other sludge?

Your biggest wins won’t come from single power choices; they will come from Power Flow, a thousand small choices won at Momentality and compounded over time.

Life is won when you turn power choices into power flow and show up even when you don’t want to.

Negative compound asymmetry Actions reveal priorities; words reveal wishes.

If easy is your god and you continually assault your future with negative force multipliers, then let me be the first to tell you: kiss your life goodbye. I’ll say it again for those in the back: You. Will. Never. Succeed.

Future You, who eventually becomes Present You, always pays for the sins of Past You.

5. Inevitability

The last and final moment. Your death is the single, inevitable moment that marks the end of your story.

“We’re all gonna die anyway, so why not enjoy the ride?” This is the philosophy of fools. The justification for weaving a life of prolonged, self-inflicted misery.

Your Lifespan is how long you live. Your Healthspan is how long you live well.

Using a distant, single moment of death to justify a bad behavior now is a fool’s gamble.

The waystone of awareness: The origin of power and energy

Every victory and defeat in your life stems from the command of your Awareness.

Observe your thoughts as if you were a third-party commander scanning the battlefield.

Every hard choice is born from awareness. And the ultimate paradox? Awareness itself is the hardest choice of all, because all energy, all reality, flows from where you choose to focus it.

Awareness is the epicenter of power.

If you don’t command your Awareness, you don’t command command your energy. If you don’t command your energy, you don’t command your decisions. If you don’t command your decisions, you don’t command your power.

Your waystone took you to the rooms you chose to visit.

When you don’t think about your thoughts, your orb of Awareness dims, and your feral mind commands it into dark places.

Your mind does this because you don’t command awareness.

Each room you visit leaves a memory, a metal box. A pile of boxes creates stronger magnetism toward that room, making it easier to return and harder to leave.

The waystone is your Awareness. Command it, and you master the castle. Surrender it to your rage, your phone, or your culture-and you become it’s servent.

Command it to illuminate the spires of gratitude and the halls of purpose.

You are the commander of your Awareness. The tragedy is not unawareness but that your Waystone has been commandeered. Highjacked, And if you want power and energy to make decisions, you must retake your waystone.

The ambushed Awareness

An ambushed Awareness is what we call “getting triggered”.

The true danger isn’t the initial trigger itself but the energy and power that follow, which manifest as poor decisions while your awareness remains imprisoned.

An ambushed Awareness can erupt into an unforgettable tirade unleashed on your child, a verbal assault that sears into their brain, creating a lifelong identity or self-esteem crisis, all because they flunked some European geography quiz. Sixty seconds of childhood trauma can define an entire life.

The ambushed Awareness is less about the event and more about the eventuality that can persist for hours or decades.

The embezzeled Awareness

Distraction.

Being completely unaware of your own Awareness.

Make technology serve you, or you will serve technology.

The linchpin isn’t your smartphone; it’s the maturity and discipline of the person using it. And because the drug of Easy is running the show, we can expect that maturity and discipline won’t prevail. Bottom line? If tech itsn’t working for you, you are working for it.

The unspoken contract: Permission vs Theft

There is a huge difference between attention given and awareness stolen.

Either you are in command or you are being controlled.

12 day Willpower bootcamp

The more you exercise willpower, the stronger it becomes.

Willpower is a muscle. It’s not gone, it’s just shriveled and weak.

Make your bed each morning for 12 days.

Finish what you start You are training your brain to close loops. Unfinished tasks drain mental energy. Finishing them boosts willpower and kickstarts the motivation feedback loop.

Defeat the notification army Turn off all non-essential notifications on your phone and computer.

The resized awareness Do one push-up or deep knee bend every hour you are awake. Strengthens the muscle for following through on what you said you would do.

Kill multitasking No multitasking. No background TV, phones, or multiple browser tabs. WHen working or eating, focus solely on the task or meal to improve concentration and mindfulness.

Multitasking divides awareness, shortchanging each task.

Breath in silence Take 5 minutes each day to sit alone, away from phones and other distractions, and count your breaths to 10.

Trigger a new story Treat every ambushed Awareness as a cue. Say, :This is a willpower rep," then re-narrate for improvement.

Direct the silence Direct your Awareness to the spires of gratitude for 5 minutes.

Go the extra mile Go the extra mile in every task. Don’t just do your job; excel.

Fosters the attitude of excellence in life, how you do anything is how you do everything.

The gauntlet Smartphone detox. Turn your smartphone into a dumbphone. Only the essentials.

North Star Offensive

When a single purpose becomes the unwavering focus of your awareness.

Without a purpose, your Waystone, the glowing sphere of your awareness, drifts dimly and aimlessly inside the castle of your mind, drawn to shiny distractions and comfortable lies. But when the train is coming, your Waystone snaps into focus. It becomes a laser beam locked on a single target: the solution.

The problem is, there is no problem.

A problem focuses your Waystone: a purpose focuses your willpower.

The gun

I put a gun to your head and offered you $10M to change that behavior by tomorrow. You would find the willpower in a heartbeat - And it wouldn’t even be that difficult.

The reality is that you don’t lack the power: you lack a compelling reason to unleash it.

When there is no gun to your head or a $10M windfall waiting, what purpose will force you to tap into your latent power? And when will you take it seriously?

Passion doesn’t inspire courage; it follows it.

The distribution of power

Pyrrhic victories

A phantom battle is wasted energy spent on a meaningless hill.

Your command rank

You’re either a commander or a conscript.

Conscript Believes that outside forces determine life’s conditions.

By believing you are a powerless pawn, you become one.

Commander Commander thinks differently and conquer hills of strategic importance in the realms of Momentality and Eventuality. These hills represent health, competence, and contribution. Commanders believe they can shape their destiny through the power of awareness and free will. Successes and failures aren’t dismissedas luck or fate; they arise from a consistent attack on the hills that matter. This heuristic puts you in the General’s tent.

Your locus of control A Commander has an internal locus; a Conscript has an external one, relinquishing power and becoming a pinball in a global machine.

Whichever command rank you hold dear is the reality you face.

Three theaters of war

The theater of..

Power Where the war is won. Everything you control or influence through your decisions.

Intelligence Preparing and planning for things you can’t control.

Chaos Everything beyond your control. Energy put here invites more chaos.

Do not expect the world to conform to your beliefs or personal codes. Or else you will be miserable.

Master decision power

The hard choice flywheel

Choosing hard is choosing right. Each hard choice makes the next one easier. Like a flywheel. Momentum does the heavy lifting. Choosing hard guarantees you are making the right choice. Quit overthinking.

Responsibility

Deliberate, reasoned, calculated, unemotional choices.

WARP Analysis W: What is the worst case outcome? AR: What’s the Asymmetric Risk profile? P: How can I rig the probability?

Reason If hard choices damage health and happiness, it’s not grit; it’s insanity.

Delay major decisions if you are feeling emotional to make smarter, more rational decisions.

Commanding emotions is about refusing to let your hijacked awareness make bad choices. Rather than bottling them up.

Courage

Bold choices that live beyond fear. And beyond you comfort zone.

Misery is born from fear overpowering courage. Failure hurts for a minutes but regret lasts for life and poisons your future.

Taking risks with hard pays off with clarity, peace, and wisdom. Even when you fail you win with growth.

Think of the most badass version of yourself and channel that person when you need courage.

Ask yourself, what would {{ insert some expert at the problem you are having here }} do in this situation?

Picture yourself on your deathbed. Will you regret this decision?

Fear is courage knocking. Easy keeps the door closed. Hard answers it.

Imagine the worst case scenario, ask yourself, what would you do next?

TO help build your courage muscle, put yourself in small skirmishes that requires courage.

Visualize yourself succeeding. Then do the thing.

Discipline

What you should do but don’t want to. To become the who you want to be.

“When you bypass the struggle, you bypass the character growth required to keep the spoils.”

Discipline turns into routine when “things I don’t want to do” become “thngs I must do for future me”.

Discipline is resisting with a struggle.

Routine discipline is resisting without a struggle.

Discipline is a muscle that grows the more you push it.

Decision comes from the latin decidere and means “to cut off”. Pick a path and you are cutting off another.

This is called a shadow transaction.

When you choose easy now the shadow transaction is hard later. When you choose hard now the shadow transaction is easy later.

Look at what shadow transaction you are making when making a choice.

Ask yourself, if I repeat this choice for 90 days, what will the outcome be? What outcomes will stem from that outcome?

Commit to doing something 90 times, no matter what.

Anchor yourself to a new identity. So discipline becomes easy.

Give yourself rules to follow in regards to bad or good habits.

Don’t forget to look how far you’ve come and how much you’ve grown. Record your wins. Pit past you against present you instead of comparing yourself to others. Ask yourself how past you would react to your current struggles.

Smallest Minimum Gain (SMG) Strategy
Break an impossible task down into small and winnable daily units.

Make things inconvenient for your worst self for when your willpower breaks down. Make it hard for “worst you” to sabotage.

Discomfort

Sacrificing short term comfort.
